Delete your account

Request deletion of your Peacemaker account and the server-side data associated with it.

Request account deletion

What we delete

  • Your Convex Auth user and active sessions.
  • Your OCR entitlement and RevenueCat customer record.
  • Your account-linked OCR usage, quota, and operational records.

What remains on your device

Bills, people, item details, totals, saved groups, and preferences are stored locally. Remove them through Bill History, clear The Peacemaker’s app storage, or uninstall the app.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting your account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Google may retain purchase records under its own legal obligations.

Timing and limited retention

We aim to complete verified requests promptly and normally within 30 days. We may retain limited records where required for legal, billing, fraud-prevention, or security obligations, as described in our Privacy Policy.
