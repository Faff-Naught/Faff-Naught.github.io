Support

Help with receipt scanning, subscription access, split totals, privacy, or a treaty that has gone temporarily awry.

Contact

Email thepeacemaker@faffnaught.com. We aim to respond within two business days.

What to include

  • Your device model and Android version.
  • The Peacemaker app version.
  • What happened, what you expected, and whether you were signed in.
  • A screenshot if useful, with receipt and personal details removed.

Purchases

For missing OCR access, include whether you purchased or restored access. Do not send payment-card details. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled through Google Play.

Account and local data

For account deletion, use the dedicated deletion request. Bill history and party details remain local to your device and can be removed inside Bill History, by clearing app storage, or by uninstalling.
